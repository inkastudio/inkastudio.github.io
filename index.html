INKA STUDIO
CORPORATE GOVERNANCE & RECRUITMENT POLICY

Effective Date: June 2026
Entity Status: Pre-Incorporation / Independent Studio
Contact: support.inkastudio@gmail.com

1.0 OVERVIEW

This document outlines the standard operating procedures regarding employment, compensation, and equity distribution for InkaStudio (hereafter referred to as "the Company"). By submitting an application or investment inquiry, the corresponding party acknowledges and accepts the terms outlined herein.

2.0 RECRUITMENT & ENGAGEMENT POLICY

2.1 Open Applications

The Company operates on a continuous, unsolicited application model. Individuals seeking engagement with the Company must submit a formal application, including a resume, portfolio, and explicit declaration of their domain specialty (e.g., Programming, 3D Modeling, Audio Engineering) to the official Company contact address.

2.2 Deferred Compensation (Revenue Share)

All current developmental and operational roles within the Company are structured as compensation-deferred agreements.

2.2.1
Remuneration for services rendered is contingent upon the successful commercial release and subsequent revenue generation of the Company's primary project.
2.2.2
Specific revenue-share percentages will be negotiated and formalized via a separate, binding legal agreement upon the applicant's acceptance into the Company.

3.0 EQUITY & INVESTMENT POLICY

3.1 Shareholder Distribution

The Founder retains a primary eighty percent (80%) equity stake in the Company. The remaining twenty percent (20%) is allocated to a reserved equity pool for early-stage capital investors and select core administrative partners.

3.2 Restricted Access

Core shareholder positions, administrative roles, and equity disbursements are strictly by invitation only, managed exclusively by the Founder. The Company does not publicly offer shares or equity positions to the general applicant pool.

3.3 Formal Investment Inquiries

Parties seeking to acquire equity outside of a formal invitation must submit a speculative investment proposal.

3.3.1
Inquiries must be directed to the official Company contact address with the strict subject line protocol: Investment Inquiry - [Entity/Individual Name].
3.3.2
The Company reserves the right to review, accept, or dismiss any speculative investment inquiries without obligation for further correspondence.

4.0 DISPUTE & APPEAL PROCESS

The Company's decisions regarding recruitment, equity allocation, and project management are final. If an applicant or external party wishes to formally request a review of a declined application or investment proposal, a formalized request may be submitted to the Company contact address. Such requests will be reviewed at the sole discretion of the Founder and administrative staff.
