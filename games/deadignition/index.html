DEAD IGNITION

SURVIVE THE NIGHT. FIX THE RIDE.

WATCH TRAILER

Your engine died. The radio is silent. And the shadows are moving.

Dead Ignition is a zero-budget, high-tension survival horror experience built for Android. Stranded in an abandoned industrial zone, you must scour the darkness for vehicle parts to repair your car. But you are not alone.

Manage your flashlight battery, hide from what stalks you in the fog, and collect the tools needed to escape before the night consumes you entirely.
