The Grid Failed. The Rules Changed.

Dead Ignition is a zero-budget, high-tension atmospheric experience. No hand-holding, no auto-saves. Just you and the dark.

1

The Breakdown

Stranded in a massive, desolate industrial zone far from civilization. The radio emits static, and the shadows are shifting. Your only escape is the car you arrived in, but vital parts are missing.

2

Scavenge & Repair

Cautiously explore abandoned warehouses, rusted scrapyards, and bloodstained offices. Find spark plugs, fuel, and wiring to rebuild your engine. Every second your flashlight is on drains the battery.

3

Survive the Locals

Every noise matters. Manage your inventory, move quietly, and hide when you have to. The locals aren't human anymore, and they are hunting you through the dark.
