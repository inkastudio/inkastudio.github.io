THE ENGINE DIES. THE NIGHTMARE BEGINS.

You are stranded. Your vehicle has broken down in a massive, desolate industrial zone far from civilization. The radio emits nothing but static, and the shadows just outside your headlights seem to be shifting.

Dead Ignition is a zero-budget, high-tension survival horror game designed for Android. No hand-holding. No auto-saves. You must step into the dark and face what lies within.

SCAVENGE. REPAIR. SURVIVE.

Your only escape is the car you arrived in, but vital parts are missing. You must cautiously explore abandoned warehouses, rusted scrapyards, and bloodstained offices to find the spark plugs, fuel, and wire you need to repair your engine.

Every noise you make matters. Every second you leave your flashlight on drains the battery. Gather supplies, manage your inventory, and hide from the locals—because they aren't human anymore, and they are hunting you.
