About Us

We are an independent game development studio built on a zero-budget, DIY ethos. We believe in raw gameplay, atmospheric immersion, and cutting out the corporate bloat.

From playful physics shooters to deeply disturbing survival horror, we build worlds we want to get lost in. We handle everything from engine architecture to marketing in-house. Based in India, operating globally.
